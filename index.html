Features

Everything the Bike For Egg Script Does

Built for one job — collecting eggs on your bike as efficiently as possible, with zero manual input.

🥚

Auto-Farm Eggs

Automatically rides to active egg spawns, collects each egg, and loops the route so your inventory fills while you're away.

🚲

Instant Bike Respawn

Detects when your bike is destroyed or despawned and instantly respawns it, eliminating the downtime that slows egg farming.

⚡

Speed Toggle

Flip a single hotkey to boost your bike's movement speed for faster route completion, then toggle back to normal at any time.

📍

Teleport to Egg Spawns

Jump directly to any active egg spawn point from the built-in list instead of riding across the map manually.
